# Volunteer Pilot Email — Draft

**From:** Alanna (cc: Ben)
**To:** [5-10 invited volunteers]
**Subject:** Help us test the new Sunshine App — you're invited!

---

Hi everyone,

As some of you know, we've been building an app for Sunshine to help us manage our growing visit program. Right now we coordinate everything through spreadsheets and email — and while that's gotten us this far, it's not going to scale as we keep growing. The app is designed to make it simpler for volunteers to find visits near them, sign up, and stay on top of their compliance documents — all in one place.

We've reached the point where we need real volunteers to try it out, and you're part of a small group we've picked to be the first. Your feedback will directly shape what this looks like when we roll it out to everyone.

**What we're asking:** Spend 20-30 minutes over the next week going through the app and giving us your honest feedback. You don't need to be tech-savvy — in fact, it's just as helpful to hear from people who find things confusing as from those who breeze through it.

### Getting Started

1. **Go to [APP URL]** and create an account
2. **Fill in your profile** — use your real information (name, location, travel distance, etc.)
3. **Add your dog's information** — name, breed, photo
4. **Upload your compliance documents** — your VSC certificate and your dog's vaccine records (photos or scans are fine)

After you register, we'll need to approve your account before you can see visits. We'll be checking throughout the day, so you should be approved within a few hours.

### Once You're Approved

We've set up a handful of test visits in the app (these are not real visits — just realistic examples for testing purposes). Here's what we'd like you to try:

5. **Browse available visits** — look at what's near you, check out the details
6. **Sign up for 2 visits** that have open spots
7. **Try joining a waitlist** — look for a visit that's already full and join the waitlist
8. **Cancel one of your registrations** — remove yourself from one of the visits you signed up for
9. **Try it on your phone** — open the app on your mobile device and poke around

### After You've Tried It

Please fill out this short feedback form (5 minutes): **[FORM LINK]**

If you run into any issues or have questions along the way, feel free to reply to this email or reach out to Ben at [BEN'S EMAIL] — he built the app and can help with any technical issues.

**We'd love to have your feedback by [DATE — 1 week from send].**

Thank you for helping us make Sunshine better — we're excited to finally get this into your hands.

Alanna

# Feedback Form Questions

For the Google Form:

1. Was creating your account and setting up your profile straightforward? If not, what was confusing?
2. Were you able to upload your compliance documents (VSC, vaccine records) without issues?
3. Could you find and sign up for visits easily?
4. Did you receive email notifications (signup confirmation, calendar invite)? Were they clear?
5. Did you try the app on your phone? How was the experience?
6. Was anything confusing, broken, or missing?
7. How does this compare to the current process of coordinating visits through spreadsheets/email?
8. On a scale of 1-5, how likely would you be to use this app regularly for managing your visit schedule?
9. Any other comments, suggestions, or things you'd like to see?
